Our go-to flight from Dallas, Fort Worth to Incheon, South Korea

The boeing that made me love the color light ,
The color that matches a bright sky,
I took so many pictures of the clouds
Where my mind escaped and made me feel farther away from my body.
I had many nights where the economy seats would be my bed, but I always
felt like I was wrapped around like a hug.
It’s a bit strange but I love the feeling of being in the air.
One step closer to being close to where my parents used to live.
One step closer to my other identity.
