An airplane that connected me from Dallas, Fort Worth to Tokyo, Japan

A boeing with two stories, always economy class, but had always brought me joy sitting in those seats.
We have a children’s hand towel as a reminder that we flew with them, a gift from our travels
I’m reminded of my child-like wonder, when I turned to my dad and simply said,

“They have foot rests! We never got those!”

It makes me laugh to myself, being amused with a simple feature that they recently installed,
but also made me remember how I treated everything as a new experience, no matter how big as a plane ride,
or as small as a foot rest.
